## 👤 About Me

I'm a **Data Science student at the Institute of Management Sciences (IMSciences), Peshawar**. I enjoy the full journey of working with data: designing the database, cleaning and querying it, analysing it, building dashboards that people can actually use, and training machine learning models on top.

My work sits across three areas:

- **Data & Databases** – schema design, advanced SQL, data warehousing, ETL-style workflows
- **Analytics & BI** – Power BI and Excel dashboards, DAX, KPI tracking, storytelling with data
- **Machine Learning** – predictive modelling, explainability (SHAP), and deploying models with Streamlit

I'm currently building towards **AI engineering** and looking for remote entry-level roles as a **Data Analyst, Data Scientist, or AI/ML Engineer**.
